# UAB texture templates

The inherited permission for textures in this folder allows their use in any mod or modpack, regardless of license or source visibility, provided the original authors receive credit. These textures are exempt from additional restrictions that might otherwise be inferred from the project's LGPL code license. Credit may be supplied on a credits page or in an accompanying text file. This grant does not relicense separate Mojang reference assets elsewhere in the repository.

## Authors

- Iron Shulker Box templates: Roadhog360, with credit to the original texture authors.
- Deepslate ores, raw ores and raw-ore blocks: Mojang, Roadhog360, Moe, DarkBum, GMG (GregTech Forum Member) and AstroTibs.
- Raw tin, silver, lead and aluminum items: AstroTibs.
- Raw tin, silver, lead and aluminum blocks; raw mythril, nickel and platinum items and blocks: GMG.
- Boron, zinc and magnesium ore, deepslate-ore, raw-item and raw-block textures: Moe.
- Remaining adapted ore templates: Roadhog360, heavily edited from Mojang textures.
- Palette-swappable oak boat texture: DarkBum.
- Separate moss layer: DarkBum and Roadhog360.
